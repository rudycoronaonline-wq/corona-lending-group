Be Prepared

What to Gather Before You Apply

A pre-approval is only as strong as the information behind it. Having these documents ready makes the process faster, cleaner, and more competitive in a multiple-offer market.

Rudy will walk you through exactly what's needed for your situation — some programs require less, some require more.

Get Your Pre-Approval →
💼 Income & Employment
📄 Last 2 years W-2s or 1099s
📄 Last 2 years tax returns (all pages)
📄 30 days recent pay stubs
📄 Self-employed: 2 years business returns + P&L
🏦 Assets & Down Payment
🏦 2–3 months bank statements (all pages)
🏦 Investment & retirement account statements
🏦 Gift letter (if using gift funds for down payment)
🪪 Identity & Property
🪪 Government-issued photo ID
🏠 Landlord contact (if renting) for verification
📋 Current mortgage statement (if refinancing)
